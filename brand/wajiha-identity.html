Why they match: turquoise is made at the same lightness and strength as the orange. Only the hue differs, so neither shouts over the other.

Never side by side as big areas. Each is a touch. Two big blocks of both at once look loud.

Contrast, measured: orange 6.5:1 and turquoise 7.2:1 on black. On ivory, turquoise deep 5.4:1 works for text; orange deep 3.8:1 is for shapes only.

05

Rules

Keep a margin around the mark equal to a third of its width. Smallest: 16 px for the mark, 80 px for the mark with the name.

How we write
  • Plain words, short sentences.
  • The name is “Wajiha Kit” in English and «واجهة» in Arabic. Always with “Kit” in English: other companies already use “Wajiha” alone.
  • The technical name wajiha-kit is for commands and files only.
  • Arabic is simple Modern Standard, not dialect.
Don’t
Don’t stretch it
Don’t move the menu to the left
Don’t swap the two colours
Don’t add shadows or glow
